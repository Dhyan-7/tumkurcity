City Profile

The Gateway to Central Karnataka

Flanked by misty granitic hills, verdant coconut groves, and major expressways, Tumakuru serves as the quintessential link between historic Karnataka and modern technological progress.

Kalpataru Nadu

Famed for its sprawling coconut, arecanut, and silk farms, Tumakuru supplies premium coconuts and copra across the nation, earning the moniker of the 'Kalpataru' (wish-fulfilling palm) land.

Knowledge Capital

Home to the renowned Siddaganga Institute of Technology, Tumkur University, medical institutions, and schools that educate hundreds of thousands of scholars from every corner of India.

Clean Green Energy

Anchored by the colossal Shakti Sthala in Pavagada taluk, generating 2,050 MW of solar power, making Tumakuru an international benchmark for renewable energy production.

Industrial Hub (CBIC)

As part of the Chennai-Bengaluru-Mumbai Industrial Corridor, the Vasanthanarasapura Industrial Area and Mega Food Park host premier automobile, FMCG, and aerospace ventures.

World-renowned pilgrimage center founded in the 15th century. Revered for Dr. Sri Sri Shivakumara Swamiji's 'Trividha Dasohi' tradition providing free education, boarding, and meals to over 10,000 students daily.

Devarayanadurga (DD Hills)

Enchanting hill fortress surrounded by deep green reserves. Features the hilltop Yoga Narasimha and foothill Bhoga Narasimha temples, panoramic viewpoints, and invigorating trek paths.

Nature & Myth 14 km from City

Namada Chilume

Perennial rock spring believed to have been punctured by Lord Rama's arrow to draw water for his tilak. Nestled inside a serene forest reserve with a deer park and herbal arboretum.

Daily: 8:00 AM - 5:30 PM

The celebrated birthplace of legendary master sculptor Amarashilpi Jakanachari. Features a breathtaking monolithic black-stone Chennakeshava idol sculpted with intricate Hoysala details.

Daily: 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Innovation & Governance

Tumakuru Smart City Mission

Selected under the Government of India's Smart Cities Mission, Tumakuru is pioneering intelligent urban governance, digital connectivity, and sustainable infrastructure.

01

Integrated Command & Control Centre (ICCC)

Real-time surveillance, automated traffic signal management, solid waste tracking, and central emergency response across the corporation.

02

Intelligent Transit System (ITS)

Modern bus terminals, electronic passenger information displays, automated smart parking zones, and e-rickshaw corridors.

03

Amanikere Eco-Restoration

Complete ecological rejuvenation of the prime catchment lake, solar-powered lake fountains, biodiversity park, and recycled water treatment.

04

Vasanthanarasapura Industrial Node

Spanning thousands of acres on the Chennai-Bengaluru Industrial Corridor, housing Japan-India Industrial Park, EV manufacturers, and textile clusters.

Flavors & Traditions

The Spirit & Taste of Tumakuru

Immerse yourself in authentic Karnataka hospitality, famous breakfast delicacies, and vibrant annual celebrations.

Tumkur Thatte Idli

Legendary plate-sized steamed idlis with a pillowy, soft texture, smothered in pure desi ghee, served with spicy red chutney and fragrant potato-coconut saagu. A must-try culinary treasure at Kyatsandra on NH 48.

Signature Food

Kalpataru Tender Coconuts

Renowned across South India for sweet water and thick malai. Tumakuru's APMC market is one of the largest copra and tender coconut trading exchanges in the country.

Agricultural Pride

Siddaganga Fair & Chariot Festival

The annual car festival and agricultural exhibition attracts lakhs of devotees and farmers. Tumakuru also celebrates Dasara, Karaga, and DD Hills Rathotsava with traditional grandeur.

Travel & Logistics

Reaching Tumakuru

Conveniently connected to Bengaluru and major south Indian metropolitan hubs via high-speed expressways and rail.

By Highway (Road)

Located on National Highway 48 (NH-48), just 70 km from Bengaluru (approx 1 hr 15 mins via elevated expressway). Frequent KSRTC Airavat, Rajahamsa, and express buses ply from Majestic (Kempegowda Bus Station) every 5 minutes.

  • Bengaluru → Tumakuru: 70 km
  • Hubballi → Tumakuru: 340 km
  • Mysuru → Tumakuru: 140 km

By Railway

Tumakuru Junction (TK) is a key railway node on the South Western Railway network with regular suburban MEMU trains and superfast expresses connecting to Bengaluru City, Yesvantpur, Shivamogga, and Belagavi.

  • Daily Suburban MEMU to Yesvantpur
  • Siddhaganga Intercity Express
  • Vande Bharat corridor connectivity

By Air (Nearest Airport)

Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) is approximately 85 km away. Accessible directly via the Dobbaspet-Doddaballapur bypass road, avoiding central Bengaluru traffic in about 90 minutes.

  • Distance: 85 km (~90 mins)
  • Prepaid Taxis & Flybus connects available
  • Smooth multi-lane bypass road access
